Changed: in focus mode, a nested list has a `+` after its blocks that adds one at its end. An empty list always shows it; a list with blocks shows it while its block, or one of its own blocks, is selected. A click beside the blocks of a nested list selects the block that holds it, its fields in the inspector, and no longer the list alone. Under the stage, _Add a block or type /_ is a button.
