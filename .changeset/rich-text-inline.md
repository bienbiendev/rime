Added: `RichTextInline` from `rimecms/panel`, a rich text edited where a block render draws it: the editor and its controls over the form's value, no fieldset, no label. The editor's typography is the same in a field and in place, with a plain variable behind each choice, `--font-text`, `--font-heading`, `--font-size`, `--font-weight`, `--line-height`, `--margin-bottom` and the others, set on the editor for every element or on one element. The field keeps its boxes in a stylesheet of its own.
