Changed: a blocks field in the form is one card of compact rows, each block closed to its grip, its icon and its name. A click on a row opens its fields under it, another closes it, and the browser remembers the open ones. A block's ⋯ menu opens it in the editor, moves it up or down, duplicates or deletes it. _Open the editor_ sits on the label's line, _Add a block_ under the card. In the blocks editor, the types to add are tiles, two a row, their picture or icon above their name, and _Type / to add a block_ sits under the blocks.
